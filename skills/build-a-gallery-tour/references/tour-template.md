## Audience adjustments

| Audience | Stops | Look for | Tone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Young children | 3 | One thing to find or count, in a short sentence | Concrete nouns, no dates beyond "very old" plus the recorded date in the facts |
| Older children and teens | 3 to 5 | A question about how it was made or used | Direct, one comparison between stops |
| Adults | 4 to 6 | A comparison or a detail of technique from the medium line | Plain, with the record facts first |
| A class or lesson | 4 to 6 | A discussion question per stop, labelled as a question | Add one line on what the records do not say |
| Online or slideshow | Any | Something visible in the image, only when a public domain image link exists | Order by date or argument, no gallery line needed |

## Rules for the text

- Facts come from the record. Background from general knowledge is allowed for famous works when labelled "Background:".
- Never state how an object looks unless the record or a labelled background sentence supports it.
- Keep attribution words such as "attributed to" and "after".
- One looking prompt per stop. More than one dilutes it.
- Do not rank the objects or call one the most important.
