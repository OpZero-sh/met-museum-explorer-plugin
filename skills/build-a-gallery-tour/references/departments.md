## Names differ between the list and the records

The `Department` line on a record does not always match the list. Seen in live results:

| Id | Name in the list | Name on records |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | American Decorative Arts | The American Wing |
| 8 | The Costume Institute | Costume Institute |
| 15 | The Robert Lehman Collection | Robert Lehman Collection |
| 21 | Modern Art | Modern and Contemporary Art |

When you tell a visitor which department an object belongs to, use the name on its record.

## Matching a request to a department

| The visitor says | Likely department |
| --- | --- |
| Mummies, pharaohs, hieroglyphs | Egyptian Art |
| Knights, swords, samurai armor | Arms and Armor |
| Greek vases, Roman statues | Greek and Roman Art |
| Old master paintings, Dutch or Italian painting | European Paintings |
| Impressionist or later painting | European Paintings for the nineteenth century, Modern Art for the twentieth and later. Search both when unsure |
| Fashion, dresses, designers | The Costume Institute |
| Prints, drawings, etchings | Drawings and Prints |
| Chinese, Japanese, Korean, South or Southeast Asian art | Asian Art |
| Medieval Europe | Medieval Art, and The Cloisters |
| Mesopotamia, Assyria, ancient Iran | Ancient West Asian Art |
| American furniture, silver, quilts, American painting | American Decorative Arts (records say The American Wing) |

These pairings are a starting point. A subject often appears in several departments, so check the `Department` line on what comes back.

## Two departments that need a caveat

- **The Cloisters.** Objects in this department are shown at The Met Cloisters, a separate museum site in northern Manhattan. A gallery number on such a record refers to that site. Do not put Cloisters stops and Fifth Avenue stops in the same visit without saying they are in different places.
- **The Libraries.** This department was not exercised in testing. Check the record before promising a gallery number.
